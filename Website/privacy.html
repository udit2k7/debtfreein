Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

1. Information Collection & Storage

DebtFreeIn operates with privacy-by-design. Sensitive credentials (such as Upstox OAuth Access Tokens and API Keys) are stored securely using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and synced to isolated Firebase Firestore documents (`system_config/upstox_auth`) solely for background execution.

2. Financial Data Handling

Virtual paper trading ledgers, expense entries, and credit card calculations remain local to your app database or secure user space. We do not sell or share personal trading history or financial data with third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Market data and execution orders are routed through official Upstox API v2 endpoints. Artificial intelligence reasoning is processed by Groq Llama 3.3 and DeepSeek R1 models via encrypted API requests.
