Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

DebtFreeIn ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit the DebtFreeIn website or use the DebtFreeIn mobile application, and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

Account and Data Deletion Instructions

In strict accordance with Google Play Store policies and global data privacy standards, users maintain full control over their account and personal data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Users can request account and data deletion at any time by emailing support@debtfreein.com. Upon receiving your request, all associated user data (including your Firestore records, authentication credentials, Auth records, simulated portfolio balances, and transaction history) will be permanently wiped within 7 days.

3. Information We Collect

Because DebtFreeIn is strictly a simulated paper-trading and financial education environment, we DO NOT collect sensitive real-world financial data. We do not ask for, nor do we store, your bank account details, Social Security Numbers, KYC (Know Your Customer) documents, or live brokerage API keys.

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Data: Email address, username, and authentication tokens (managed securely via Google Firebase).
  • Simulation Data: Your paper-trading transaction history, saved AI quantitative strategies, simulated portfolio balances, and interactions with the AI models.
  • Device and Usage Data: IP addresses, device identifiers, operating system types, app crash reports, and analytics data regarding how you navigate the application.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to operate the simulation engine and provide the services described:

  • To create and maintain your simulated trading account.
  • To process and display your simulated trades and AI engine interactions.
  • To improve our algorithms, user interface, and overall user experience.
  • To monitor for technical anomalies, crashes, and unauthorized usage.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

5. Third-Party Data Processors

To operate the Platform, we share necessary technical data with trusted third-party infrastructure providers. These include:

  • Google Firebase & Google Cloud: Used for secure database hosting, user authentication, and crash analytics.
  • AI Service Providers (e.g., Google Gemini): Used to process quantitative logic and chat prompts. Note: We do not send personally identifiable information (PII) to AI models.

We DO NOT sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party marketers or data brokers.

6. Data Security

We have implemented measures designed to secure your personal information from accidental loss and from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. All information you provide to us is stored on secure servers behind firewalls, utilizing industry-standard encryption protocols.

7. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Platform after we make changes is deemed to be acceptance of those changes.
